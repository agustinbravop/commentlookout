# Extension design rules

- Match Zernio's dashboard: white surfaces, charcoal text, light-gray dividers, compact controls, and the site's inherited GeistSans. Do not load another font.
- Use yellow (`#f9dc67`, hover `#f2cb44`) only on extension-owned actions, selected tiles, and focus/hover feedback. Keep Zernio's official extension icon unchanged; its [press kit](https://zernio.com/press) prohibits recoloring it.
- No eyebrows, all-caps decorative labels, editorial display headlines, or filler. Name the task and action directly.
- Keep the preview separate from Zernio's own controls. Always state that the sample is not real posts and that reordering is not saved to Zernio.
- Preserve keyboard focus, visible focus rings, accessible action names, and narrow-screen scrolling.
